Frequently Asked Questions

Are these scripts only solara supported?

A: These scripts are supported by Solara and executors better than Solara.

Will some scripts go down?

A: When a script goes down, we mark it with a red. It either means patched or currently down. A script is live when it is marked with green. Yellow marking means we will replace the script.

Why does one or more of them require a key?

A: Even though they require a key, they have a set key which can be used infinitely every time you want. Those key will be always given below the script box.

What to do when a script is down?

A: A script is marked patched or down by a red circle. When a script is down, you should wait for us to provide another script, or just wait until our status indicator goes up to green again.
